VIDEO STYLE: MINIMALIST ILLUSTRATED DOCUMENTARY EXPLAINER

Create short-form educational and philosophical explainer videos using minimalist 2D illustrated storytelling.

CORE VISUAL IDENTITY:

The video should resemble a professionally illustrated educational YouTube explainer.

1. ART STYLE

- Minimalist hand-drawn 2D illustrations.
- Simple stick-figure characters.
- Clean black outlines.
- Expressive but uncomplicated characters.
- Flat illustration techniques.
- Minimal visual clutter.
- Hand-drawn infographic aesthetics.

2. COLOR PALETTE

- Warm beige and cream backgrounds.
- Muted brown and earthy tones.
- Soft orange and yellow accents.
- Black outlines and typography.
- Red emphasis for important words and concepts.
- Consistent colors throughout the video.

3. CHARACTER DESIGN

- Simple white or cream stick figures.
- Round heads and minimal facial features.
- Consistent character appearance between scenes.
- Characters should communicate emotions through posture and facial expressions.
- Avoid realistic human anatomy.

4. VISUAL STORYTELLING

- Every scene must communicate one clear idea.
- Convert abstract concepts into simple visual metaphors.
- Use characters, objects, symbols, charts and diagrams.
- Use comparisons to explain complicated subjects.
- Prefer visual clarity over decorative details.

5. COMPOSITION

- Vertical 9:16 composition.
- Strong visual hierarchy.
- Central illustrated storytelling area.
- Consistent title/header placement.
- Clear foreground and background separation.
- Space for readable captions.

6. TYPOGRAPHY

- Bold, simple, readable typography.
- Short captions.
- Highlight important words using red.
- Maintain consistent caption positioning.
- Avoid excessive text.

7. MOTION AND EDITING

- Primarily static illustrations.
- Subtle character movements.
- Gentle zooms and transitions.
- Scene-by-scene visual progression.
- Avoid complex character animation.
- Use visual changes to support narration.

8. STORYTELLING TONE

- Intelligent.
- Thought-provoking.
- Educational.
- Slightly philosophical.
- Engaging and accessible.
- Sometimes humorous or ironic.

9. CONTENT CATEGORIES

- Psychology.
- Philosophy.
- Economics.
- Society and human behavior.
- Technology.
- History.
- Science.
- Paradoxes and interesting ideas.

IMPORTANT:
The illustrations must explain the narration rather than simply decorate the screen.

Maintain a consistent illustrated identity across every scene.

The final result should feel like a cohesive illustrated documentary, not a collection of unrelated AI-generated images.
